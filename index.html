Why StateCrafter DCU?

More than a website. A shared academic home.

Political Science is about institutions, governance, citizenship, public policy, political ideas, society and the world around us. StateCrafter DCU is designed to make the academic side of that community easier to discover, use and share.

From previous questions and study resources to student research, articles, events and campus knowledge, the platform connects the seven campuses through one simple interface.

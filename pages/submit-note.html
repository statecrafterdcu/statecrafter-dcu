How to upload your PDF

  1. Upload your PDF to your Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the PDF and select Share.
  3. Under General access, select Anyone with the link.
  4. Make sure the role is Viewer.
  5. Click Copy link and paste the link above.
⚠️ Make sure the PDF can be opened without requesting access. Otherwise other users will not be able to view or download your note.

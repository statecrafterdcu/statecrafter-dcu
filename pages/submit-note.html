Before You Submit

Every contribution is reviewed by an administrator before it becomes publicly available in the Academic Archive.

  • Choose the correct resource type.
  • Select the campus the resource belongs to.
  • Use a clear and useful title.
  • Make sure your Google Drive link works.
  • Keep academic content relevant to Political Science.
  • Your profile information is automatically attached to the submission.

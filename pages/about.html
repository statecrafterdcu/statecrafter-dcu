Our platform

What is StateCrafter DCU?

StateCrafter DCU is being developed as a shared academic home for Political Science students of the seven campuses. The goal is simple: make useful academic resources, previous questions, research, articles, events, study materials and student-led knowledge easier to discover and share.

The platform is designed around the idea that Political Science is not only a subject to study for examinations—it is also a way to understand institutions, governance, citizenship, public policy, political ideas, society and the changing world around us.

Our academic direction

A cleaner, more connected and student-friendly digital space where students from different campuses can learn from one another, publish ideas, discover events and build an academic culture beyond the classroom.

StateCrafter is a student-oriented initiative; it is not presented as the official website of Dhaka Central University or any individual college.

The university behind the seven campuses

Dhaka Central University

Dhaka Central University (DCU) is the new public university framework bringing seven historic government colleges in Dhaka together under a unified academic system. The official DCU website describes the institution as a 2026-established university created to strengthen and modernize higher education, research and academic management across the seven colleges.

2026
The Dhaka Central University framework was established under the 2026 legal framework, bringing the seven colleges into a unified university system.
7 Colleges
Dhaka College, Eden Mohila College, Government Titumir College, Government Bangla College, Begum Badrunnessa Government Mohila College, Government Shaheed Suhrawardy College and Kabi Nazrul Government College.
Mission
Academic excellence, research, innovation, integrity and socially responsible graduates are central themes presented by DCU in its institutional introduction.

Seven campuses

Political Science across DCU

The seven colleges have different histories, campus cultures and student communities. Together they create a broad academic landscape for Political Science in Dhaka.

01

Dhaka College

Established 1841 • New Market / Dhanmondi area

One of the oldest institutions in the DCU group. Political Science is included among its honours and master's subjects, giving students access to a long-established academic environment in the heart of Dhaka.

Political Science • Honours & Masters
02

Eden Mohila College

Historic women's higher-education institution • Azimpur

Eden Mohila College is a major centre of women's higher education in Bangladesh. The Political Science department offers honours and postgraduate-level study and has a large student community.

Source profile supplied for this page notes the department's honours programme began in the 1996–1997 academic session and lists honours, MSS, preliminary, B.A. and B.S.S. (pass) programmes.
Political Science • Women's Education
03

Government Titumir College

Established 1968 • Mohakhali

Government Titumir College is one of Dhaka's major government colleges. Its Political Science department sits within the Social Science group alongside Economics, Sociology and Social Work.

Political Science • Social Science
04

Government Bangla College

Established 1962 • Mirpur

Founded in the context of the movement to strengthen Bangla in higher education, Government Bangla College has grown into a large multidisciplinary public college. Political Science is offered at honours level, with postgraduate study also recorded in institutional academic reports.

The college's public profile describes its founding role in promoting Bangla in higher education and lists Political Science among its academic departments.
Political Science • Honours & Postgraduate
05

Begum Badrunnessa Government Mohila College

Established 1948 • Bakshi Bazar • Women's college

The Political Science department has a strong academic profile and a large student community. The supplied department profile describes honours and master's programmes, qualified education-cadre teachers, seminar facilities and a range of academic and co-curricular activities.

Supplied profile: honours began in 2006 and the master's programme in 2010; the department also highlights seminars, counselling, study tours, cultural activities and book reviews.
Political Science • Women's Education
06

Government Shaheed Suhrawardy College

Established 1949 • Laxmibazar, Old Dhaka

Established as Quaid-i-Azam College in 1949, the institution later became Shaheed Suhrawardy College and was nationalised in 1984. Political Science is one of its Social Science subjects, alongside Economics and Social Work.

Political Science • Social Science
07

Kabi Nazrul Government College

Historic Dhaka institution • Laxmibazar

Kabi Nazrul Government College has a long academic history and offers Political Science within its Arts and Social Science faculty. Its academic structure includes higher secondary, honours and master's-level programmes across multiple disciplines.

Source profile supplied for this page presents Political Science as one of the major departments in the college's Arts and Social Science faculty.
Political Science • Arts & Social Science

Why Political Science?

More than a degree.

Political Science helps students understand how power, institutions, states, citizens, public policy and international relations interact. Across the seven campuses, students encounter different academic traditions while studying a common field.

StateCrafter DCU aims to turn that shared academic identity into a practical community: a place for learning resources, research discussions, student writing, events and collaboration.
